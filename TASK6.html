World Map Explorer

Welcome to World Map Explorer! Click on different countries on the world map to explore information about them.

Explore Different Countries

Image Map Shapes Used

  • Circle: Used for India.
  • Rectangle: Used for United States.
  • Polygon: Used for Australia.
  • Default: Covers all remaining areas of the image.

How to Use

1. Click on the circular region to explore India.

2. Click on the rectangular region to explore the United States.

3. Click on the polygon region to explore Australia.

4. Click anywhere else on the map to explore the world.
